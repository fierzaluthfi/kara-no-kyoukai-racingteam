FACTORY PLATFORM BASELINE

YAMAHA NEW JUPITER MX 135 (5-SPEED) TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Standard factory mechanical architecture powering the RSMX135 racecraft, delivering benchmark 135cc agility, high-revving liquid-cooled endurance, and dual disc stability.

ENGINE ARCHITECTURE 4-Stroke, SOHC, 4-Valve, Liquid Cooled Forward-inclined single cylinder with Yamaha DiASil Cylinder & lightweight Forged Piston
DISPLACEMENT 134.4 cc (54.0 × 58.7 mm) Compression Ratio: 10.9 : 1 for high-density combustion and responsive throttle pickup
MAX OUTPUT & TORQUE 12.52 PS @ 8,500 RPM Peak Torque of 12.14 Nm @ 6,000 RPM providing punchy midrange acceleration
TRANSMISSION & CLUTCH 5-Speed Manual (1-N-2-3-4-5) Constant mesh 5-speed transmission paired with a wet multi-plate manual clutch
FUEL & INDUCTION Mikuni VM22 (Old Jupiter MX Spec) Classic Old Jupiter MX mechanical carburetor setup with high-flow jetting for crisp, instantaneous analog throttle response
CHASSIS & SUSPENSION Diamond Steel Underbone Rigid steel diamond frame with telescopic front forks and rear linked monoshock swingarm
BRAKING SYSTEM Dual Hydraulic Disc Brakes Front double-piston floating caliper disc + Rear single-piston hydraulic disc brake
WHEELS & TIRES Marchesini 5-Spoke (F) & Y-Spoke MotoGP Prototype (R) Front: 17" Marchesini 5-spoke with Michelin 90/80-17 (Soft Compound) | Rear: 17" Honda Sonic-based Marchesini Y-spoke GP Prototype with Michelin 100/80-17 (Medium Compound)
